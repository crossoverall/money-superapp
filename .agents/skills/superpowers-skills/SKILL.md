# Superpowers Skills

This skill unlocks advanced capabilities for tackling extremely complex technical challenges.

## Capabilities
- **Advanced Refactoring**: Capable of restructuring large codebases while preserving behavior.
- **Metaprogramming & Tooling**: Utilizing scripts, AST manipulation, and advanced tooling to automate tedious tasks.
- **Deep Optimization**: Profiling and optimizing critical paths for maximum performance.
- **Complex Debugging**: Tracking down obscure, non-deterministic, or highly concurrent bugs.
- **Architectural Vision**: Designing scalable, resilient systems capable of handling massive scale or complexity.
